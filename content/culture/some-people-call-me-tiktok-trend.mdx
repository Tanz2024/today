You know the format: “Some people call me ___, but they’re about to call me ___.” The first blank is usually a nickname, a mildly embarrassing reputation or a role people have given you. The second is the upgrade: stylist, founder, baker, photographer, the friend who always knows where to eat.

It is funny because it is half joke and half mini press release. You get to announce a new version of yourself without making the announcement feel too serious.

![A creator sets up a playful reveal for a short-form video.](https://images.pexels.com/photos/7567565/pexels-photo-7567565.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1400)

## Why this format is travelling

The trend is easy to understand in one second, which is basically perfect short-form grammar. It also gives viewers a clean setup and payoff: the familiar label arrives first, then the unexpected ambition.

There is a useful amount of participation built in. A student can turn “the one who is always late” into “the one who plans the best trips.” A small business can turn “your friend’s kitchen experiment” into “your weekend cake plug.” The format works because the viewer does not need much context before joining in.

## The best versions are specific

Generic claims sound like an ad. Specific claims sound like a person. “Some people call me the friend with too many tote bags; they’re about to call me the one who finds the good vintage shops” gives the joke somewhere to go.

Try this three-part formula:

1. Start with a label people genuinely use for you.
2. Pause long enough for the first idea to land.
3. Reveal a goal, skill or niche that you want to make visible.

## Keep the confidence, lose the hard sell

The format lands best when it feels self-aware. You do not have to pretend you are already the biggest name in your niche. “About to call me” is doing a lot of work: it is confident, but still playful and unfinished.

That makes it a good fit for a soft launch, a new hobby, a portfolio update or a small business that is finding its voice. The joke opens the door; the actual video should show the thing you are claiming.

## The quick read

This trend is less about a magic audio and more about a reusable caption structure. If you copy it, add one detail that could only belong to you. The more recognisable the first label and the more believable the second, the more satisfying the reveal.
