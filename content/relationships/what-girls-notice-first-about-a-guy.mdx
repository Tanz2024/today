There is a whole internet economy built around the question: what do girls notice first about a guy?

The honest answer is less dramatic than a viral “secret.” People usually notice whether you look cared for, whether you seem comfortable in your own skin and whether being around you feels easy. The goal is not to become a different person. It is to remove the avoidable distractions.

![Young man in a relaxed smart-casual outfit](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1617127365659-c47fa864d8bc?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1400&q=85 "A relaxed outfit usually reads better than an overworked one")

## First: the overall impression

Before somebody analyses your haircut or trainers, they get a general read. Do you look like you made a little effort? Do your clothes fit? Do you seem present in the conversation instead of performing for an invisible audience?

That first read happens quickly, but it is not a final verdict. A good first impression opens the door; how you act keeps the conversation going.

## Grooming usually beats expensive style

Clean hair, fresh breath, tidy nails and clothes that do not look like they lost a fight with the floor are doing a lot of work. None of this requires a luxury shopping trip.

If you want a simple reset, focus on the basics: shower, deodorant, moisturiser if your skin needs it, clean shoes and a haircut that you can maintain. Be so fr: a RM500 jacket cannot rescue questionable hygiene.

## Smell is memorable — keep it close, not loud

Fragrance can become part of someone’s memory of you, but more is not automatically better. One or two sprays that sit close to the skin usually feel more intentional than a cloud that arrives before you do.

If you are choosing a scent, try it on skin and wait for the dry-down. Citrus, clean musk and soft woods are easy starting points, especially in warm weather. The best fragrance is one people discover when they are near you.

## Style says something, but it does not need to shout

People notice whether an outfit looks like it belongs together. That can mean a clean T-shirt, straight trousers, simple sneakers and one detail you actually like. It does not need to be designer or perfectly on-trend.

![Clean sneakers and casual clothing laid out for an outfit](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1523398002811-999ca8dec234?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1400&q=85 "Fit and coordination matter more than a logo")

Fit matters more than a logo. A basic outfit that sits well often looks more polished than a complicated one full of pieces you keep adjusting.

## Confidence is not being the loudest person in the room

Confidence is often quieter: making eye contact without staring, asking a question and listening to the answer, ordering without apologising for existing. It is being able to laugh at yourself without turning every moment into a performance.

Trying too hard is not a moral failure. Everyone gets nervous. The useful move is to stop treating nerves like proof that you are failing.

## The part people remember after the outfit

How you speak to a waiter, how you respond when somebody disagrees and whether you give people room to talk can change the whole impression. Good manners are not a dating tactic; they are evidence of how you move through the world.

So, what do girls notice first? Usually a combination of care, comfort and energy — not one magical feature. And what do guys think they notice? Often the same things, just with more group-chat debate.

## Brisa’s take 👀

Look clean, smell good, wear clothes that feel like you and be kind without expecting a medal. That is already a stronger glow-up than copying a random “alpha” checklist.
