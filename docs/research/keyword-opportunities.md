# Keyword and product opportunities

Research date: 14 November 2026. Numeric search volume, ranking difficulty and traffic are **unverified** because no Google Keyword Planner, Search Console, Ahrefs or Semrush export was available. These are intent-led opportunities, not volume claims.

## Scoring rubric

Each opportunity receives a 1–5 score for usefulness, commercial intent, differentiation, feasibility, repeat usage and evidence confidence. Competition is deliberately not scored as “low” without data; it is described qualitatively from the observed SERP/product landscape.

Priority score = usefulness + commercial intent + differentiation + feasibility + repeat usage. Evidence confidence is reported separately.

## Research constraints

- No keyword is labelled low competition.
- No numeric search volume or revenue estimate is asserted.
- “Commercial intent” means the page can plausibly lead to a useful product, affiliate disclosure, or software comparison; it does not imply conversion.
- Paid provider costs are unknown until a model, token budget, upload size, and retention policy are selected.

1. AI professional email writer
