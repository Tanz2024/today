# SEO opportunity ranking

This ranking uses the rubric in `keyword-opportunities.md`. It is a product prioritization tool, not a forecast of rankings or traffic.

| Rank | Opportunity | Why now | Product dependency | Main risk |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI professional email writer | Clear outcome, bounded text input, strong examples and repeat use | Provider abstraction and limits | Generic output unless templates are useful |
| 2 | AI prompt improver | Low upload/privacy complexity and easy before/after demonstration | Provider or deterministic baseline | Crowded category; needs differentiated workflow |
| 3 | AI code explainer | Strong alignment with TodayAI’s AI audience | Server-side model call and code-size limits | Incorrect explanations must be framed carefully |
| 4 | AI workflow planner | Connects editorial, tools and commercial-intent content | Form schema and optional model refinement | Avoid presenting generated plans as expert advice |
| 5 | AI flashcard generator | Repeat use and education relevance | Export format and bounded inputs | Quality varies by source text |
| 6 | AI PDF summarizer | High utility but meaningful demand and privacy potential | Safe upload, provider, deletion, cost control | Sensitive documents and high request cost |
| 7 | Human-or-AI game | Shareable, fixed-cost entertainment | Dataset and content rights | Misleading “AI detector” claims |
| 8 | AI word detective | Low-cost daily loop | Precomputed embeddings/dataset | Needs original puzzle quality |

Official Google guidance supports helpful, people-first content and warns against scaled low-value content. Tool pages should therefore be complete products with explanatory content, not thin keyword wrappers.
