# Ad placement policy

## Principles

- Ads must be visually distinct from navigation, inputs, output, downloads and game controls.
- No ad may overlap gameplay or create an accidental-click path.
- Do not place ads in a loading/error state where users may mistake them for the requested result.
- Use fewer placements on interactive tools and games than on long-form editorial pages.
- Keep commercial links labelled and separate from editorial recommendations.

## Route guidance

- Articles: consider a limited in-content placement only after readable content and before secondary interactions.
- Tools: place ads outside the primary input/output workflow.
- Games: place ads outside the game stage and verify current Google policy before release.
- Admin, authentication, community submission and private pages: no ads.

## Verification

Test at phone, tablet and desktop widths with keyboard and touch input. Record screenshots and confirm that ads cannot be confused with buttons or game controls.
